## ❌ Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Generic Applications**
   - ❌ Don't: Apply to same job description for 10 companies
   - ✅ Do: Customize based on company & role

2. **Delayed Follow-up**
   - ❌ Don't: Wait for company to contact you
   - ✅ Do: Follow up after 48-72 hours

3. **Weak LinkedIn Profile**
   - ❌ Don't: Use casual photo, vague description
   - ✅ Do: Professional photo, clear value proposition

4. **No Networking**
   - ❌ Don't: Rely only on job portals
   - ✅ Do: Network actively, build relationships

5. **Unprepared Interviews**
   - ❌ Don't: Wing it in technical interviews
   - ✅ Do: Practice and prepare thoroughly

6. **No Follow-up After Interview**
   - ❌ Don't: Wait silently
   - ✅ Do: Send thank you email within 24 hours
